1. Executive Summary & Healthcare Problem Definition

In modern multi-specialty hospitals and medical outpatient departments (OPDs), physical waiting lines and chaotic waiting rooms cause significant patient distress, unmonitored infection risks, and physician schedule unpredictability. Patients often wait 2 to 4 hours with no visibility into their consultation sequence, leading to crowded lobbies and high missed-turn rates.

CareFlow is an enterprise-grade native Android healthcare application engineered to transform outpatient hospital throughput. It replaces physical queues with Real-Time Digital Token Passes, Dynamic Wait-Time Estimation Telemetry, One-Tap Doctor Summoning with High-Urgency Audio-Visual Call Alerts, and a centralized Hospital Command Center for multi-department operations.

4. Cloud Firestore Database Schema

1. /hospitals/{hospitalId} (Healthcare Institutions)
Stores hospital master records: id, name, opdName, address, city, phone, email, adminId, adminEmail (single-admin authority), adminName, adminPhone, departmentIds[], createdAt, updatedAt.

2. /doctors/{doctorId} (Physicians & Specialists)
Stores credentialed clinicians: id, userId, name, specialization, departmentId, departmentName, hospitalId, qualification, experienceYears, consultationFee, avgConsultationMinutes, shiftStartTime, shiftEndTime, maxTokensPerDay, isAvailable, isInOPD, loginEmail, password.

3. /appointments/{appointmentId} (OPD Consultation Bookings)
Stores live consultation passes: id, patientId, patientName, patientPhone, doctorId, doctorName, departmentId, departmentName, hospitalId, tokenNumber, date, timeSlot, status (PENDING, CALLED, IN_CONSULTATION, COMPLETED, CANCELLED, ABSENT), notes, symptoms, bookedAt.

4. /queues/{doctorId_date} (Daily Live Queue Telemetry)
Stores active queue state: doctorId, date, currentToken (currently serving), totalTokens, status (WAITING, ACTIVE, PAUSED, CLOSED), breakMessage, updatedAt.

5. /users/{userId} (User Profiles & Role Binding)
Stores user accounts: id, name, email, phone, role (PATIENT, DOCTOR, ADMIN), hospitalId, selectedHospitalId, fcmToken, createdAt.

6. /doctor_reset_requests/{requestId} (Doctor Security Reset Requests)
Stores clinician self-service password reset requests: id, doctorId, doctorName, doctorEmail, hospitalId, otp, status (PENDING, VERIFIED, DISMISSED), requestedAt, expiresAt.

5. Multi-Role Profile Isolation & Single-Admin Invalidation Architecture

CareFlow incorporates two enterprise-grade security protocols addressing complex healthcare access patterns:

Protocol A: Multi-Role Account Isolation Under One Email
A healthcare worker can simultaneously be a hospital administrator, a consulting doctor, and a patient under the exact same email address (e.g. doctor@hospital.com). CareFlow guarantees strict isolation by qualifying accounts via composite tuple keys (email, portalRole). Logging into the Patient Portal creates/binds a dedicated Patient profile (patient_...) with appointments and medical history; logging into Doctor Portal binds the clinician chamber; logging into Admin Portal verifies administrative authority. Sessions and data are completely segregated with zero cross-contamination.

Protocol B: Strict Single-Admin Invalidation Engine
To prevent orphaned administrative access, each hospital designates exactly one authoritative adminEmail on the hospital record. When the administrator updates their email in Admin Settings: (1) hospitals/{id}.adminEmail is atomically updated, (2) all previous admin user documents in Cloud Firestore are immediately downgraded from ADMIN to PATIENT, and (3) any login attempt with the previous email into the Admin Portal is immediately rejected with an explicit access-denied error.
